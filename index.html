2. Description of Service

Calorie Counter & Food Log is a food logging and nutrition tracking application designed to help users record what they eat and monitor calories and nutrients. The App may provide features such as:

  • Daily food diary with calorie and macronutrient tracking
  • A personal food library where you create and manage your own products
  • Tracking of up to 37 nutrients (macronutrients, vitamins, and minerals)
  • Barcode scanning with product lookup via the Open Food Facts database
  • Calorie and macro goals with a built-in goal calculator
  • Calendar view with daily statistics and period averages
  • Meal reminders via local notifications
  • Optional iCloud sync of your data across your own devices
  • Multi-language support (35 languages)

By using the scanner, you acknowledge that:

  • Barcode lookup requires an internet connection

  • Retrieved data only prefills a product form; nothing is saved unless you save it
  • The free tier includes a limited number of successful barcode scans; Premium removes this limit

The App displays attribution to Open Food Facts within the scanner interface.
